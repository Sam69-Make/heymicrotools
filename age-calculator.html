How to use the age calculator

It takes about ten seconds. You only need a date of birth, and the results update as you type, so there is no calculate button to hunt for.

  1. 1

    Enter the date of birth. Tap the first field and choose a date from the picker, or type it in. On a phone you will get your device’s native date wheel.

  2. 2

    Choose a comparison date, if you need one. The second field starts on today’s date. Change it to see how old someone was on a past date, such as a job start or a wedding, or how old they will be on a future one.

  3. 3

    Read your results. You will see the exact age in years, months and days, the same age expressed as total weeks and total days, and how many days remain until the next birthday.

  4. 4

    Copy or start over. Press Copy result to paste a plain-text summary into a form or message. Press Reset to clear the birth date and return the second field to today.

Frequently asked questions

How does the calculator handle leap years and February 29 birthdays?

Every leap day between the two dates is counted, so the total days and total weeks are exact. Years and months are counted as whole calendar months from the birth date, and the leftover days are what remains after that. If you were born on February 29, the calculator treats March 1 as your birthday in years that have no leap day.

Can I find my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Set the “Age at the date of” field to any date on or after the date of birth. This is handy for checking eligibility on a deadline, such as school admission cut-offs, or for working out how old someone was when something happened. The next-birthday countdown is measured from whichever date you choose.

Is my date of birth stored or sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser. The dates you enter are not uploaded, saved or shared, and they are gone as soon as you close or refresh the page.
