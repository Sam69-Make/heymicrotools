Quick answers

How fast will I hear back?

We reply to most messages within 24 to 48 hours. If you don’t see a reply, check your spam folder, since replies come from our support address.

How do I request a custom calculator?

Choose Suggest a New Tool in the form. Tell us what you want to work out, which numbers you would enter, and an example with the answer you expect. The more specific you are, the faster we can build it.

What should I include in a bug report?

Name the tool, the values you entered, the result you got and the result you expected. Mentioning your device and browser helps too.
