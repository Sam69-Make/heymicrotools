How to use the word counter

There is nothing to set up. Here is how most people use it.

  1. 1

    Add your text

    Type straight into the box or paste something you have already written. The numbers at the top change with every keystroke, so there is no button to press.

  2. 2

    Check against your limit

    Writing for an essay, a tweet or a meta description? Use the word count or the character count that matches the limit you were given. Most platforms count spaces, so look at the "with spaces" figure first.

  3. 3

    Tidy it up

    Use the buttons above the box to switch everything to capitals or lowercase, or to remove double spaces left behind by copying from a PDF or email. Made a mistake? Press Undo.

  4. 4

    Copy it out

    Press Copy Text to put the finished version on your clipboard, or Clear Text to start again with a blank box.

Frequently asked questions

How does the counter work out words, sentences and paragraphs?

Words are any groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks, so "well-known" counts as one word. A sentence ends at a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark, and each line break also starts a new sentence. Paragraphs are the lines of text separated by a line break. Abbreviations like "e.g." can make the sentence count a little higher than you expect.

How is the reading time calculated?

We divide your word count by 238 words per minute, a typical silent reading speed for adults. It is an estimate. Slower readers, technical writing and text in a second language will usually take longer.

Is my text saved or uploaded anywhere?

No. The counting happens in your browser, and this page does not upload or store what you type or paste. Once you close or refresh the page, the text is gone, so copy anything you want to keep.
