Small tools that do one job and get out of your way.

heymicrotools is a collection of free calculators and everyday utilities. They open fast, run in your browser, and work the same on a phone as they do on a laptop.

What heymicrotools is

Most of us need a quick calculation or a small file fix a few times a week. How old will someone be on a certain date? What percentage is this discount? Can this photo be under 100 KB for a form? These are small questions, and they shouldn’t require an account, an app download or a page full of pop-ups.

So we make tools for exactly those moments. Each one does a single thing, shows the answer as you type, and keeps the page clean. You land on it, get your result, and leave.

The tools are free to use. There is no trial period and no feature locked behind a plan.

What we stand by

Three rules shape every tool we publish.

Everything stays on your device

Calculations and image processing run in your browser. The numbers you type and the files you choose are never sent to our servers, because we built the tools so they don’t need to be.

No sign-up, ever

You don’t need an email address, a password or a profile. Open a tool and use it. If a tool ever needs something from you, it will be because the job requires it, not because we want your data.

Simple on a small screen

We design for the phone first. Fields are large enough to tap, the right keyboard opens for each input, and nothing needs pinching or zooming. The desktop version is the same page with more room.

How we build tools

A calculator is only useful if you can trust its answer. Before a tool goes live, it goes through the same steps.

  1. 1

    Start with one clear job

    We decide what question the tool answers and leave out everything that doesn’t help answer it. A short page is easier to use and easier to check.

  2. 2

    Test for accuracy

    We check results against worked examples done by hand and against known values. We also try the awkward cases on purpose: leap days, zeros, negative numbers, very large and very small values, and empty fields.

  3. 3

    Test for speed

    Tools should respond as you type. We keep pages light, avoid heavy libraries and try them on ordinary phones, not only on a fast desktop.

  4. 4

    Explain the result

    Where it helps, we show the formula and a worked example next to the tool, so you can see how an answer was reached and check it yourself.

  5. 5

    Keep listening

    If you find a wrong result or a rough edge, tell us. A real example from a real person is the fastest way for us to fix something.

A note on accuracy: our tools are built for everyday estimates and quick checks. For decisions about your health, taxes, legal matters or large amounts of money, please confirm the result with a qualified professional.
