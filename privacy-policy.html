The short version

  • What you type into our tools and the images you choose stay on your device. We never receive or store them.
  • Like most free websites, we show ads and measure traffic. That involves cookies and basic, non-identifying usage data.
  • You can say no to personalized ads, and you can contact us about your data at any time.

This policy explains what information is handled when you use heymicrotools (“we”, “us”, “our”) at heymicrotools.com. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here. If you disagree with any part of it, please stop using the site.

1. Local browser processing

Our calculators and utilities are built to run entirely inside your web browser. This is the most important privacy feature of the site, so we want to be specific about it:

  • Numbers, dates and text you enter into a tool (for example in the Age, Percentage or BMI calculators) are calculated on your device.
  • Images and files you select (for example in the Image Compressor) are read and processed by your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Results are generated on your device and are not saved by us.

Because this data never reaches our servers, we cannot see it, store it, sell it or share it. If a tool ever remembers a preference, it does so only in your own browser’s storage, and you can clear that at any time through your browser settings. When you close or refresh a page, anything you entered is gone.

Please note that the web pages themselves, and the fonts and scripts they need, are delivered to you by us and by third-party services. Those services can see standard technical details about your visit, as described in the sections below, but they do not receive the contents of your tool inputs or files from us.

2. Log files and analytics

Like most websites, we and our hosting and content-delivery providers may automatically collect standard technical information when you visit. This can include:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version, device type and operating system
  • The pages you view, the date and time of your visit, and how long you stay
  • The website that referred you and the pages where you leave

We use this information in aggregate to keep the site secure, fix errors, understand which tools people find useful and improve performance. It is not linked to your name or other personally identifying details, and we do not use it to identify you individually.

We may use third-party analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to help with this. These services may set cookies or use similar technologies to recognize returning browsers and produce usage statistics. Where the law requires your consent for this, we ask for it first.

Our pages also load some resources from third parties, such as fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from a content-delivery network. When your browser requests these, the provider receives your IP address and basic browser information, as it would on any website.

3. Cookies and web beacons

Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. We and our partners use them for three purposes:

  • Essential: to make the site work and keep it secure.
  • Analytics: to count visits and understand how the site is used.
  • Advertising: to show ads and measure how they perform. See section 4.

You can control cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers let you view, block or delete cookies, and you can usually choose to be warned before one is set. Blocking cookies may affect how some parts of the site or its ads behave, but our tools will continue to work. For step-by-step instructions, visit allaboutcookies.org.

Web beacons

Web beacons (also called pixels or clear GIFs) are tiny, usually invisible images placed in a page. Analytics and advertising partners may use them, often together with cookies, to count visitors, recognize returning browsers and measure whether an ad was displayed or clicked. Web beacons do not read information from your device.

4. Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners

We use Google AdSense to display ads on this site. These ads help us keep the tools free. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads on our site.

  • Google as a third-party vendor. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • DART cookie. Google uses the DoubleClick DART cookie, among other advertising cookies, to help serve and measure ads across the sites where it operates. The DART cookie does not collect personally identifying information such as your name or email address.
  • Other ad partners. Other third-party ad servers or ad networks may also use cookies, JavaScript or web beacons to deliver and measure ads. We do not have access to, or control over, the cookies these third parties use.

How to opt out of personalized ads

  • Visit Google Ads Settings to turn off personalized advertising or adjust the ads you see.
  • Visit aboutads.info/choices to opt out of personalized advertising from other participating third-party vendors.
  • Adjust your browser settings to block or delete advertising cookies, or use your device’s ad-privacy controls.

Opting out does not remove ads from the site. You will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to your browsing activity. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, read Google’s explanation.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: we ask for your consent before cookies or similar technologies are used to personalize ads, in line with applicable law and Google’s requirements. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time.

5. GDPR and CCPA privacy rights

Because our tools process your inputs locally, we hold very little personal data about you. The limited information we do handle, such as analytics and advertising identifiers and messages you send us, is covered by the rights below.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland (GDPR)

We rely on consent for advertising cookies and, where required, analytics cookies, and on our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving a free website for basic server logs. You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy
  • Correct inaccurate data, or have data erased
  • Restrict or object to our processing of your data
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing
  • Complain to your local data protection authority

If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, to request deletion or correction of it, to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, and not to be treated unfavorably for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. However, the use of advertising cookies by our ad partners may be considered “sharing” or a “sale” under California law. You can opt out through Google Ads Settings, through your browser’s privacy controls, or by emailing us.

How to make a request

Email support@heymicrotools.pages.dev with the subject “Privacy request” and tell us which right you want to use. We may need to confirm your identity before acting. We will respond within one month, or within 45 days for California requests, as the law requires.

Children’s privacy

heymicrotools is a general-audience site and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site, our partners or the law change. When we do, we will revise the date below. We encourage you to review this page from time to time.
